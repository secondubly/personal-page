I guess this a personal blog now 😅

I had a bit of an epiphany this past week - against my better judgment I decided to export my job application tracker to a spreadsheet so I could see the overall progress on my job hunt. The results were sobering. Since February of this year, I have applied to over 1,000 jobs and am genuinely no closer to getting hired than I was when I started this process back in *2025*. This tracker doesn't even have the jobs I had applied to last year either (as I deleted those applications for a fresh start).

Upon reading that number (exactly 1,054 to be precise) I speedrun through the five stages of grief in record time, but not before mentally spiraling. Thankfully, I also have a therapist that I could speak to about the issue, and she recommended that I do something that is going to be extremely hard for me.

No job-related work for the rest of the year.

Until January 1, 2027, I am not allowed to look at job applications, talk about job applications, or even think about applying anywhere 😮‍💨

My life has been on a bit of a downward spiral since I was laid off and it has done an absolute number on not just my mental health, but my self-confidence, my relationships, and my general outlook on life. But for as long as I can remember, work is all I've really known - having something to work *towards*. And now it just feels like I'm in freefall; nothing I try has been working, reaching out to coworkers, friends, family, none of it has really led much of anywhere, and with each passing day, my belief that I am ever going to crawl out of this hole just shrinks and shrinks and I turn more and more into a zombie.

So I'm done.

For the next 3 months, I will not be looking at Linkedin, answering any job related emails (I will be automating my responses), nor talking or thinking about job related things.

I think I'm going take up drawing, or maybe finally learn how to swim.

See you in 2027.
